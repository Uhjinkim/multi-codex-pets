# Service identification marks

Official flat marks, preserved without redrawing or recoloring:

- Codex/OpenAI: the menu bar template from the installed official app,
  `ChatGPT.app/Contents/Resources/chatgptTemplate@2x.png` (`com.openai.codex`).
- Orca: `Orca.app/Contents/Resources/app.asar.unpacked/resources/tray/orca-menu-barTemplate@2x.png`.
- Claude: https://claude.com/icon.png (official 192px site icon, flat terracotta mark).
- Antigravity: https://antigravity.google/assets/image/brand/antigravity-icon__full-color.png
  (official transparent 540px press asset; https://antigravity.google/press).

The two monochrome menu bar marks use macOS template rendering to follow the
system appearance. Colored marks keep their original colors. UI containers share
the same padding and corner-radius proportions; the marks are not cropped.

These marks identify their respective services. They belong to their respective
owners and are not covered by this project's MIT license.
